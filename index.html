Device Specifications

[SYS.01] memory

SoC & Processor

MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Pro manufactured on TSMC's 4nm node. 4x Arm Cortex-A78 up to 2.5GHz + 4x Cortex-A55 power efficiency cores with Mali-G615 GPU.

KERNEL ARCH arm64-v8a
[SYS.02] smartphone

Super AMOLED Panel

6.67-inch flexible AMOLED with 120Hz adaptive refresh rate (30/60/90/120Hz steps), 2000 nits peak brightness, 960Hz PWM dimming, and HDR10+ certified.

RESOLUTION 1080 x 2400 (FHD+)
[SYS.03] tune

Rotary Hardware Dial

Modular tactile mechanical dial with integrated click switch. Complete sysfs driver exposure for mapping to custom actions, volume stepping, or flashlight dimmer.

DRIVER NODE /sys/class/rotary_dial
[SYS.04] photo_camera

50MP Sony Optics

50MP f/1.8 main sensor paired with secondary portrait depth unit. Full Camera2 API Level 3 support with RAW10 Bayer stream capture without third-party vendor bloat.

GCAM STATUS Full Support (Zero Tint)
[SYS.05] battery_charging_full

Battery & Charging

5000 mAh high-cycle life cell with 33W USB Power Delivery 3.0. Supports 5W wired reverse charging for accessories like earphones and wearables on-the-go.

INTERFACE USB Type-C 2.0 OTG
[SYS.06] wifi

Radios & Security

Dual 5G Standalone (SA/NSA), Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.3 LE, NFC, in-display optical fingerprint scanner, and independent hardware unlock slot.

BOOTLOADER Fastboot Unlocked
